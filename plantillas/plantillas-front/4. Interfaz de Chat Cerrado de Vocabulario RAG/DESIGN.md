## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an interplay between classical Chinese material culture and modern digital minimalism. Rooted in the poise of blue-and-white porcelain (青花瓷, Qīnghuācí) and the decisive authority of red cinnabar seal paste (印泥, Yìnní), the aesthetic avoids folklore kitsch in favor of an architectural, high-precision web shell tailored for Mandarin language mastery.

### Personality & Tone
- **Scholarly yet Dynamic:** Intellectual precision balanced by gamified progression loops.
- **Architectural Harmony:** Structured layouts inspired by classical courtyard composition (quadrangle balance, purposeful voids, clean proportion).
- **Tactile Refinement:** Surfaces feel like unglazed paper and polished ceramics rather than flat synthetic plastics.

### Design Movement
The interface fuses **Contemporary Structural Minimalism** with **Editorial Ceramic Polish**. It leverages deliberate negative space, razor-thin structural borders, rice-paper tinted warmth, and precise vermilion call-to-actions to evoke confidence, focus, and cultural resonance.

## Colors

The palette derives from enduring imperial craft materials, codified into an accessible digital scheme.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary — Azul Porcelana (`#1F4E79`):** Represents structural authority, primary navigation, major titles, brand chrome, and focused learning controls.
- **Secondary — Rojo Sello (`#C8102E`):** Represents the indelible cinnabar seal. Used for critical CTAs, streak trackers, gamification milestones, review alerts, and tone marks for Tone 1.
- **Tertiary — Deep Cobalt Ink (`#0F2942`):** Highest contrast foreground text, active sidebar backgrounds, and selected data visualizations.
- **Neutral Surface — Blanco Arroz (`#F5F1E8`):** Canvas backdrop reminiscent of handmade rice paper (*Xuan* paper), reducing optical fatigue during extensive reading sessions.

### Functional & Gamification Tones
- **Porcelain Surface Light (`#FAF8F5`):** Card elevation surfaces, input backgrounds, and character canvas layers.
- **Cobalt Mist Tint (`#E8EDF2`):** Neutral dividing strokes, active row hover states, and inactive progress bars.
- **Tonal Linguistic Coding System:**
  - *Tone 1 (Flat/High):* Vermilion Seal (`#C8102E`)
  - *Tone 2 (Rising):* Jade Celadon (`#1E8266`)
  - *Tone 3 (Falling-Rising):* Imperial Ochre (`#C27D00`)
  - *Tone 4 (Falling):* Porcelain Lapis (`#1F4E79`)
  - *Tone 5 (Neutral):* Ink Smoke Grey (`#707E8B`)

## Typography

Typography bridges Western informational clarity with the optical squareness of Hanzi (汉字). 

- **UI Shell & Headlines:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric poise, welcoming terminal curves, and strong hierarchy for global app navigation, dashboard headers, and module banners.
- **Body & Instructional Copy:** **Inter** serves as the utilitarian standard for dense instructional materials, grammatical breakdowns, and vocabulary definitions.
- **Hanzi Glyphs:** Rendered via fallback stack `Noto Sans SC, PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei, sans-serif`. Characters must maintain optical balance with adjacent Pinyin and Latin translations without visual crowding.
- **Data & Numeric Statistics:** **JetBrains Mono** guarantees fixed-width alignment for learning analytics, streak counts, spaced repetition intervals (SRS), and time-tracking metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The web app shell employs an asymmetrical, persistent left-rail navigation structure combined with a flexible 12-column content workbench.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** Collapsible vertical navigation dock (80px collapsed / 240px expanded), flanked by a 12-column grid workspace with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Left-rail defaults to compact icon-only mode.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Left rail collapses into an ergonomic bottom navigation dock.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout distances favor unhurried breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl` between modular training cards), recalling classical landscape scroll arrangements where empty space (*liúbái*, 留白) actively defines meaning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic muddy dropshadows, drawing inspiration from low-sheen porcelain glaze resting on handmade fibers.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F5F1E8` (Blanco Arroz). Never receives elevation.
- **Level 1 (Dock & Primary Tiles):** Raised `#FAF8F5` surface enclosed with a 1px hairline perimeter border of `#E8EDF2`. Ambient ground shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Hanzi Trays):** Slightly lifted containers utilizing a crisply delineated border (`#D8E2EA`) coupled with an indigo-tinted shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 78, 121, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Character Inspector & Flying Menus):** Pure floating porcelain panels framed by a 1px border in `#1F4E79` at 20% alpha, with a high-diffusion elevation shadow: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural discipline with soft, tactile edges resembling water-smoothed stones or porcelain cups.

### Corner Radius System
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, character tiles, inputs) use base `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`).
- Content modules, flashcards, and modal containers use `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Floating badges, streak pill indicators, and audio triggers use fully rounded pill contours (`9999px`).
- Character canvas grids apply an interior square constraint with slightly rounded external outer bounding boxes to reflect historical character practice books (*Tianzige* / 田字格).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Vermilion Seal):** Solid `#C8102E` background, white label text, subtle 1px border in `#A00C24`. On hover: `#B00E29` with elevated shadow. Dedicated to high-value actions: "Submit Answer", "Start Session", "Complete Lesson".
- **Secondary Action (Porcelain Blue):** Solid `#1F4E79` background, white text. Used for system-level controls: "Next Item", "Dictionary Look-up", "Save Vocabulary".
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Ghost surface with 1px border in `#E8EDF2` and `#0F2942` text. Hovers transition to `#E8EDF2` at 50% opacity.

### 2. Character Practice & Study Cards (Flashcards)
- Built on `#FAF8F5` with a dual hairline border (outer `#E8EDF2`, inner 0.5px dotted `#D0DBE5` for stroke guideline boxes).
- Houses the oversized **hanzi-card-lg** typography tier, complemented by an audio pronunciation pill button and color-coded tone badge directly above the character.
- Active card hover produces a `-2px` vertical translation with an accentuated porcelain shadow.

### 3. Chips & Tone Indicators
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying Pinyin, Radical breakdowns, and HSK difficulty levels.
- Pinyin chips feature a light neutral background (`#FAF8F5`) surrounded by a 1px tonal border matching the syllable's specific tone color (e.g., `#C8102E` border and text for Tone 1).

### 4. Input Fields (Pinyin & Hanzi Prompt)
- Background `#FAF8F5` with a 1.5px structural border in `#D8E2EA`.
- Focused state applies an immediate border color shift to `#1F4E79` and a soft ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(31, 78, 121, 0.12)`).
- Dedicated Pinyin inputs automatically format tone marks dynamically with trailing tone numbers (e.g., `ni3` transforms immediately to `nǐ`).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkbox borders use `#1F4E79` at 1.5px weight. When selected, fills with solid `#1F4E79` displaying a crisp white verification glyph.
- Multiple-choice quiz rows function as wide selectable cards: unselected cards have a `#E8EDF2` border; selected states shift to a `#1F4E79` border with a pale blue-wash background (`#E8EDF2` at 40%).

### 6. Gamified Study Statistics Bar
- Embedded horizontal status strip showing current daily streak (flame icon filled with `#C8102E`), SRS review queue count, and daily Hanzi retention percentage.
- Progress meters utilize a dual-track bar: `#E8EDF2` groove paired with a high-contrast `#C8102E` or `#1F4E79` rounded progress fill.
